# Screenshot Group 22

Version: v2.7.0
Release: Delegated Intake Completion
Evidence state: approved and closed

This group connects browser uploads to the consolidated review workspace and
removes the Owner as the default reviewer for routine trusted intake. Trusted
contributors review their own batches, administrators can review ordinary
contributor batches, and Owners retain policy and exception oversight.

The workflow preserves immutable originals, explicit original-versus-edit
decisions, role-scoped access and auditable review outcomes. Ordinary family
conversation posts remain immediate, while private-message access remains a
recipient consent decision rather than an Owner approval step.
