# Family Archive v3.8.0 — Migration Qualification

Released: 2026-08-02

Version 3.8.0 closes Screenshot Group 33 and the 33-group product roadmap. It
qualifies the large-migration control plane without reading, retaining or
publishing private family photos.

## Qualification evidence

- A deterministic synthetic 30,000-entry manifest
- Sixty durable checkpoints of 500 entries
- An injected interruption after 12,000 entries and exact resume
- Three isolated failures with bounded recovery
- A rejected 500-entry replay sample
- Exact expected-versus-observed reconciliation and manifest digest

## Product state

The photo workflow, production deployment, private Wasabi storage, guided family
access, restoration, albums, maps, messaging and migration controls are complete
for this release. Screenshot Groups 01-33 are approved and closed.

## Important boundary

The qualification data is synthetic. The real 30,000-photo family migration has
not been performed and must remain a private, operator-controlled activity with
preflight, capacity confirmation, checkpoints, monitoring and reconciliation.

## Known limits

- Photo preservation is complete; other media types are not complete pipelines.
- Automatic restoration suggestions require human review and may be replaced by
  an authorized original-first manual edit.
- Google Maps, Wasabi and hosted production rely on externally operated services.
- Formal family acceptance, custodianship and recovery exercises are human work.
