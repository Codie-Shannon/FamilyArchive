# Family Archive v1.0 Custodianship

Family Archive requires a named primary custodian, a confirmed successor and an
explicit emergency procedure. Designation never grants access by itself:
normal authentication, role, branch, sensitivity and original-access policies
continue to apply.

Before v1.0 human acceptance:

1. complete the controlled family pilot and resolve blocking feedback;
2. verify production backups in an isolated restore namespace;
3. close or formally accept every integrity repair case;
4. nominate and confirm the primary and successor custodians;
5. review the threat model, secrets inventory and recovery runbook; and
6. record the acceptance run without deleting earlier blocked runs.

Emergency access must be time-bounded, auditable and followed by credential
rotation. It must never overwrite originals or erase historical evidence.

Generated fixtures, synthetic recovery metrics and repository tests can
exercise these boundaries, but they cannot stand in for real participant,
production or custodian approval.
