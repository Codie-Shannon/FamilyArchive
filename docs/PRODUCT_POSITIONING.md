# Family Archive Product Positioning

## Product Promise

Family Archive is a privacy-first, preservation-grade platform for protecting,
understanding and selectively sharing family history across generations.

## What Reviewers Should See First

1. Immutable originals, integrity verification and derivative lineage.
2. Provenance, uncertain historical facts and human-reviewed metadata.
3. Granular family access, private collaboration and auditability.
4. Duplicate intelligence, restoration review and alternate originals.
5. Cloud intake, backup, recovery and long-term custodianship.
6. Controlled public stories and privacy-reduced archive maps.

Encrypted family messaging, attachments, presence, typing and voice notes
support the archive experience. They are not the headline.

## De-emphasized Expansion

Large public servers, anonymous public posting, public DMs, voice calls,
guidance bots, broad social publishing and WhatsApp or Messenger bridges
remain disabled or de-emphasized in the portfolio narrative. They are retained
as bounded architectural expansion work, not presented as the reason the
product exists.

## Portfolio Demonstration

The portfolio environment uses only synthetic Aotearoa family history. It is
read-only after seeding, contains no production credentials and must never
connect to real family storage. The showcase demonstrates one coherent
journey: ingest, verify, review, enrich, preserve, share and recover.

The strongest evidence is a short walkthrough, architecture and threat-model
documentation, measured validation results, accessibility proof and a
successful synthetic backup and recovery exercise.
