## Access Records

- accounts record pending, approved, rejected or suspended state;
- optional family-branch membership records a claimed family connection;
- media records flag living people and children;
- original-access grants are explicit, reasoned, expirable and revocable; and
- these facts do not widen the current verified Owner-only archive boundary.

## Contributor Intake

Contributor submissions retain source context and proposed metadata without
silently promoting files into the archive. Upload templates and resumable
sessions preserve user intent and progress as separate records.

## Communication Boundary

Public conversation threads are deliberately separated from private archive
knowledge. Only approved signed-in accounts with an allowed role may post.
Locked threads reject posts, and only visible moderated messages are rendered.

Anonymous contact:

- creates no user or archive identity;
- grants no archive access;
- enters moderation in the pending state;
- stores optional reply email privately;
- stores one-way network correlation evidence for abuse handling; and
- never renders email addresses, fingerprints or correlation tokens publicly.

Public or anonymous content cannot become an accepted archive fact without the
relevant human-review workflow.
